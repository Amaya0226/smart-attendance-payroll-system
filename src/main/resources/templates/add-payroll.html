Calculation Information

  • Basic salary is loaded according to the effective salary revision.
  • Allowances and additional deductions come from active Payroll Rules.
  • Overtime is calculated from completed days and the employee's overtime records.
  • Absence deduction uses Basic Salary ÷ 30 × confirmed absent days.
  • Tax is calculated using the active TAX rule as a percentage of gross salary.
  • Today's attendance is displayed separately and excluded from payroll totals.
  • The backend recalculates payroll values when the record is saved.
